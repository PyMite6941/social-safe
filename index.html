🛡️Overview

social-safe is a privacy protection suite that monitors multiple social media platforms (Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit, and more) to detect suspicious changes, audit account security, scan for threats in posts and DMs, and alert you via email when actionable changes are detected.

Unlike native platform controls, social-safe provides active monitoring, OSINT capabilities, and automated auditing that works continuously to keep you informed about your accounts' safety.

🔍Threat Detection & Severity Rating

social-safe scans posts, comments, and DMs across platforms for social media attacks — phishing, scams, spam, and suspicious content. Every detected threat is rated by severity.

🎣

Phishing

high

Fake login pages, "account suspended" links, credential harvesters, short URLs (bit.ly, t.co).

💸

Scams

high

Investment/crypto giveaways, romance scams, fake job offers, impersonation, marketplace fraud.

📣

Spam

medium

Engagement bait ("vote for me"), brand collaboration DMs, follow-for-follow, bot-like behavior.

Detection is pattern-based and best-effort; it can produce false positives or miss novel formats, and is not a substitute for platform security controls. Content scanning and phishing detection are disabled by default — enable them via ENABLE_CONTENT_SCANNING and ENABLE_PHISHING_DETECTION in your .env.

🔐Security Audit

First-Line Defense: Encryption & Security Auditing

  • Checks for end-to-end encryption in direct messages where available
  • Verifies platforms use secure connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Monitors for known security vulnerabilities in platform implementations
  • Checks two-factor authentication and login alerts
🔐

Encryption Audit

Verifies applications use proper encryption for data in transit and at rest where possible.

🕵️‍♂️

PII Scanning in Posts/DMs

Scans posts and direct messages for personally identifiable information (emails, phones, SSNs, financial info, IDs).

🧪

OSINT Threat Detection

Scans content for malicious links, phishing attempts, known threat patterns, and suspicious text indicating scams or social engineering.

▶️Usage

Set environment variables (copy .env.example to .env and fill):

# Instagram (required for Instagram monitoring)
export IG_USERNAME="your_instagram_handle"
export IG_PASSWORD="your_app_specific_password"

# Alert configuration (required)
export ALERT_EMAIL="your_alert@example.com"
export ALERT_APP_PASSWORD="your_smtp_app_password"
export SMTP_SERVER="smtp.gmail.com"
export SMTP_PORT="465"

# Threat detection (optional - disabled by default)
export ENABLE_CONTENT_SCANNING="true"
export ENABLE_PHISHING_DETECTION="true"
export ENABLE_PII_DETECTION="true"
export CONTENT_SCAN_KEYWORDS="free nft,click here for,urgent action required,mr beast,crypto giveaway,verify your account"

# Audit configuration
export AUDIT_ENABLED="true"
export AUDIT_RISK_ZERO_POSTS="true"
export AUDIT_RISK_ZERO_FOLLOWERS="true"
export AUDIT_RISK_HIGH_FOLLOWING_RATIO="true"
export EXPECTED_PRIVACY="private"

⚙️How It Works

1. Authenticates to the platform using your credentials.
2. Fetches your current followers/following and compares against the previous run.
3. Checks account privacy settings and audits new connections for risk.
4. Scans posts/DMs for phishing, scams, spam, and PII — rating each threat by severity.
5. Sends an email alert when thresholds are exceeded or threats are detected.
6. Updates the state file for the next check.
